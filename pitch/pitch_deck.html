The Enterprise AI Conundrum: Cloud Leaks vs. Toy Scripts

Companies and governments want autonomous digital workers, but current options fail three critical enterprise requirements.

🛑
Data Sovereignty & Security Leaks

Public AI SaaS (OpenAI, Anthropic Cloud, Copilot) force enterprises to stream proprietary banking, customer, and state data to third-party servers. Regulators strictly penalize cross-border data transfers.

82% of enterprises restrict public cloud AI due to compliance risk.
🐌
Bloated Python Stacks & Fragile Scripts

Frameworks like LangChain, CrewAI, and AutoGen are written in interpreted Python. They consume 2.5 GB RAM per agent, suffer from GIL bottlenecks, ~400ms latency, and crash during multi-hour background tasks.

Unstable in production; incapable of running 10+ sub-agents reliably.
🔒
Uncontrollable Black Boxes Without Approvals

Existing autonomous tools lack granular human governance. They execute unchecked terminal commands, mutate files, and interact with external systems without cryptographic audit trails or interactive approval cards.

Executives refuse full autonomy without deterministic safety rails.
