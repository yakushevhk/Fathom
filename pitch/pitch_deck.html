🛑

Unstable in production; incapable of running 10+ sub-agents reliably.

Existing autonomous tools lack granular human governance. They execute unchecked terminal commands, mutate files, and interact with external systems without cryptographic audit trails or interactive approval cards.

Executives refuse full autonomy without deterministic safety rails.
