The Remote Workforce Crisis: High Cost, High Friction, Slow Ramp-Up

Scaling modern digital operations with human remote labor faces severe structural limitations: exorbitant salaries, months of recruiting lag, high employee turnover, timezone disconnects, and inconsistent task execution. Knowledge work has become the primary operational bottleneck for high-growth enterprises.

Human Remote Labor Pain Points
  • High Overhead: $4,000–$10,000/mo per knowledge worker + equipment, licenses, and payroll taxes.
  • Burnout & Inconsistency: Human SDRs and data staff make errors on thousands of complex rows.
  • Timezone & Availability Lag: Work halts during nights, weekends, and holidays.
  • Churn & Knowledge Loss: When staff leave, institutional memory disappears.
The Fathom Digital Solution
  • Fractional Cost: Predictable flat monthly subscription per autonomous employee seat.
  • Deterministic Quality: Every task follows verified standard operating procedures (SOPs).
  • 24/7/365 Continuous Output: Digital coworkers process inbound leads and pipelines around the clock.
  • Permanent Knowledge: All discoveries and client preferences persist forever in memory graphs.
