Relational Intelligence: Beyond Flat Unstructured Records

Enterprise intelligence is fundamentally relational. In crates/memory, Fathom structures verified facts into a directional Entity Knowledge Graph that automatically connects individuals, corporate entities, funding events, job titles, and technology stacks into a unified queryable web.

Figure 36.1: Enterprise Entity Knowledge Graph Explorer — 3-hop relationship traversal across 5,420 nodes in 1.62 ms.
Core Entity Node Classes
  • Person: Verified full name, LinkedIn URL, email, seniority.
  • Organization: Domain, headcount, funding round, revenue tier.
  • Technology: Cloud stack, CRM, web frameworks, payment APIs.
Directional Edge Types & Traversal
  • Person ──[works_at {{title, since}}]──► Organization
  • Organization ──[uses_tech {{confidence}}]──► Technology
  • 6-hop traversal queries execute in <1.8ms via SQLite recursive CTEs.
